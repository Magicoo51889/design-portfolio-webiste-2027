Side project · Karting

Off the clock, on the track.

Away from design work I'm rebuilding a 2001 TKM kart to restore, maintain and race.

I stripped the chassis bare and rebuilt it with new bearings and separate wet and dry rear axles. I then rebuilt the engine and carburettor before its first run at Clay Pigeon Raceway. The plan is to race after university.
